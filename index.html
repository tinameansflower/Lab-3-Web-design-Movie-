10 Things I hate about you

I don't typically watch movies as I do shows; However, this movie will be an all-time favourite and comfort of mine.

Although the plot is basic and practically everything is very predictable, and there are tons of stereotypes within the movie, it's such a comedic, silly, and cool old movie that it's charming. 10 Things I Hate About You is a romcom movie filmed and released in 1999. The main character is Kat, an intelligent teen who refuses to conform or change herself to meet others' expectations. She values being herself and doesn't care for the approval of guys. On the other hand, she has a younger sister who has yet to experience the romance world, daydreaming, changing, and trying to fit into the norms people have set for the 'Ideal' woman to live her teen life to the fullest and have a love-filled high school experience. The plot is about how Bianca (her younger sister) wants to date but can't because of a family rule made by her father that if Kat dates, she can; otherwise, it's not happening. So a boy who is infatuated with Bianca pays a guy to take out Kat.

As terrible as it may sound, I thoroughly enjoyed the movie, and I will always go back to it. It gives me some joy that people can still see and appreciate each other for who they are rather than who they pretend to be or rather than who they feel like they are. Everyone around Kat Stratford disliked her because she was true to herself and voiced her opinions, cursing those who put her down. She was treated unfairly, but she didn't really care; she knew her own worth, and despite everything, she and the main love interest end up finding each other. They love loudly and sillily, which expresses to the audience that people will see your worth even if you don't see yours. And those who do will appreciate and cherish you more than anyone would ever know.
